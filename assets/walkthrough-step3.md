# 🚀 Resume Instantly

The magic happens when you return to your editor.

Whenever you come back, a helpful popup will instantly remind you:
- What file and function you were editing
- How long you were away
- The very next step you were supposed to take (based on your comments)

No more *"Wait, what was I doing?"* moments. Dive straight back into the zone!
