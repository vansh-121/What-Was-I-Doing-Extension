# ☕ Step Away with Confidence

Developers are constantly interrupted. Don't worry about trying to remember your train of thought.

Whenever you step away—whether you're:
- Switching to another application
- Taking a coffee break
- Closing VS Code for the weekend

...we instantly capture a snapshot of your workspace. You don't need to do anything manually!
