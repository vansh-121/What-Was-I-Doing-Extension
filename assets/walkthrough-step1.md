# 🎯 Your Silent Productivity Assistant

Welcome to the **What Was I Doing?** extension! Your workflow is about to get a major upgrade.

Just write your code as usual. Behind the scenes, we automatically monitor:
- 📄 The specific files you're editing
- 📍 Your exact cursor position and active functions
- 📝 Any nearby `TODO`, `FIXME`, or `BUG` comments

**100% Private:** Everything is stored locally on your machine. We never send your code or data anywhere.
