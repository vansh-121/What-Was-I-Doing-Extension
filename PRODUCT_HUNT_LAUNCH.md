### **Product Hunt Description (Main Post)**

**Headline Hook:**
> Ever spent 5 minutes trying to remember what you were coding before that meeting? We've all been there. Meet **What Was I Doing?** — the silent productivity assistant that saves your mental energy.

**The Problem:**
Developers face constant interruptions:
- 🤝 Unexpected meetings pull you away
- 💬 Slack messages derail your flow
- 📞 Phone calls break your concentration
- ☕ Coffee breaks make you forget where you were
- 🌙 You close your laptop Friday and come back Monday confused

The worst part? You waste 5-10 minutes *every single time* trying to remember:
- What file was I editing?
- What function was I working on?
- What was my next step?

**The Solution:**
What Was I Doing? runs silently in the background and tracks your exact coding context. The moment you return, a helpful popup instantly reminds you of:
- ✅ The exact file you were editing
- ✅ The function you were working on
- ✅ Your TODO comments and next steps
- ✅ Your recent work history

**Why You'll Love It:**
🔒 **100% Private, 100% Local** — Your code never leaves your computer. No cloud. No servers. No tracking.

⚡ **Zero Setup** — Install it, forget about it. It works automatically.

🎯 **Hyper-Focused** — Only tracks the context you actually need.

⌨️ **One Command Away** — Search "What Was I Doing" in Command Palette for quick access.

🎨 **Fully Customizable** — Adjust idle timeout, popup behavior, history size, file exclusions, and more.

**The Impact:**
- 🚀 Get back into flow state 5x faster
- 💪 Recover your context in 1 second instead of 5 minutes
- 🧠 Free up mental energy for actual coding
- 📈 Reduce context-switching overhead by up to 40%

**Perfect For:**
- Developers with frequent interruptions
- Remote workers with async communication
- Teams with Slack/Teams culture
- Anyone who works across multiple projects
- Freelancers juggling multiple clients
